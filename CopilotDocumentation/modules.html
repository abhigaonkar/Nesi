📦 NESI Application - Functional Modules

🎯 Core Operational Modules

1. Customer Management Module

Frontend: /app/pages/customers/

Backend: CustomerControllers

Description: Complete customer lifecycle management including CRM capabilities

  • Customer List & Search: Searchable customer database with advanced filtering
  • Customer Profile: Comprehensive customer information management
  • Contact Management: Multiple contacts per customer with roles
  • Sales History: View all quotes and work orders for customer
  • Accounting Integration: AR notes, invoices, payment tracking
  • Business Unit Assignment: Multi-BU customer relationships
  • Customer Rates: Custom pricing agreements
  • Address Management: Multiple addresses (billing, shipping, service)

2. Quote Management Module

Frontend: /app/pages/quotes/

Backend: QuoteControllers

Description: Complete quotation lifecycle from creation to conversion

  • Quote Creation: Create detailed quotes with multiple line items
  • Quote Editing: Edit quotes with revision history
  • Quote Worksheet: Detailed cost estimation and pricing
  • Quote Strategy: Win/loss tracking and analysis
  • Quote Approval Workflow: Multi-stage approval process
  • Quote to WO Conversion: Convert approved quotes to work orders
  • Quote History: Complete audit trail of changes
  • Post-Mortem Analysis: Win/loss reasons and competitive analysis

3. Work Order Management Module

Frontend: /app/pages/workOrder/

Backend: WorkOrderControllers

Description: End-to-end work order execution and tracking

  • Work Order Creation: New WO from scratch or from quote
  • WO Summary Dashboard: Real-time project status overview
  • Task Management (Buckets): Organize work into tasks/phases
  • Line Item Tracking: Detailed deliverables and materials
  • History & Notes: Complete activity log and comments
  • PO Management: Associated purchase orders
  • Project Team Assignment: Assign PM and team members
  • Progress Tracking: Completion percentage and milestones
  • Scanned Documents: Attach files and photos

4. Timesheet & Labor Tracking Module

Frontend: /app/pages/timesheet/

Backend: TimesheetControllers

Description: Comprehensive time and expense tracking for payroll and job costing

  • Time Entry: Daily/weekly time entry against WO/Quote/Shop
  • Multiple Pay Types: Regular, overtime, double-time tracking
  • Project Time Allocation: Time tracked per work order/quote
  • Vacation Management: PTO requests and tracking
  • Expense Tracking: Employee expense submission and approval
  • Break Time Tracking: Compliance with labor laws
  • Per Diem: Travel allowance tracking
  • Bank Pay: Comp time and flex time management
  • Timesheet Transfer: Move time between projects

5. Purchase Order Module

Frontend: /app/pages/purchaseOrder/

Backend: PurchaseOrderControllers

Description: Procurement and vendor purchasing management

  • PO Creation: Create purchase orders for materials and services
  • PO Detail Management: Line items with quantities and costs
  • Vendor Selection: Choose from approved vendor list
  • Business Unit Allocation: Distribute costs across BUs
  • PO Approval Workflow: Management approval for large POs
  • Receipt Tracking: Track goods/services received
  • Credit Card Purchases: Track employee credit card expenses
  • PO Notes & Comments: Communication with vendors

6. Vendor Management Module

Frontend: /app/pages/vendors/

Backend: VendorControllers

Description: Supplier and vendor relationship management

  • Vendor List & Search: Searchable vendor database
  • Vendor Profile: Company information and details
  • Vendor Specialties: Categorize vendors by capabilities
  • Accounting Information: Payment terms, tax ID, banking
  • Address Management: Multiple vendor locations
  • Contact Management: Vendor contact persons
  • Phone Call Log: Track communications with vendors
  • Purchase History: All POs for vendor

7. Employee Management Module

Frontend: /app/pages/employees/

Backend: EmployeeControllers

Description: Human resources and employee lifecycle management

  • Employee List: Complete employee directory
  • Employee Profile: Personal and employment information
  • Privilege Management: Assign system permissions
  • Wage Management: Salary and hourly rate tracking
  • Employment History: Job roles and promotions
  • Reviews & Performance: Employee evaluation tracking
  • Disciplinary Actions: HR incident management
  • Termination Management: Exit processing
  • Offer Management: Job offer tracking
  • Days Off Management: PTO balances and requests
  • Contact Information: Emergency contacts

8. Applicant Tracking Module

Frontend: /app/pages/applicant/

Backend: ApplicantControllers

Description: Job application and recruitment management

  • Applicant List: Track all job applicants
  • Application Management: Store resumes and application details
  • Interview Tracking: Schedule and track interviews
  • Hiring Pipeline: Move applicants through stages

9. Reporting & Analytics Module

Frontend: /app/pages/reports/

Backend: ReportControllers

Description: Business intelligence and reporting

  • Master Quote Grid: All quotes with filtering and analysis
  • Master Work Order Grid: All WOs with status and financials
  • Master Purchases Grid: All POs and expenses
  • Job Cost Report: Project profitability analysis
  • Income Statement: P&L reporting
  • Balance Sheet: Financial position report
  • Inventory Usage: Material consumption analysis
  • Customer Rates Grid: Pricing analysis by customer
  • FVR Grid: Field verification reports
  • Timesheet Reports: Labor analysis and reporting

10. Business Unit Management Module

Frontend: /app/pages/business_unit/

Backend: BusinessUnitControllers

Description: Organizational unit configuration and management

  • BU Configuration: Create and manage business units
  • Visibility Controls: Define user access to BUs
  • Cost Allocation: Distribute expenses across BUs
  • BU Reporting: Financial reports by business unit
